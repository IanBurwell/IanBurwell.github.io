Welcome to my website's source! It isn't anything special and I am no web dev, but feel free to poke around.

## Usage
So you want to build my website? I doubt it, but for my own records you need to [install pnpm](https://pnpm.io/installation) and run:
```bash
pnpm install 
pnpm run dev
```

## A Mental Model and Manifesto
This website is separated into projects pages and blog posts. 
Blog posts should be free spirited and tell a story. Project pages on the other hand should be clean and documentary.

### Projects
Project pages document projects I have worked on, and will stay grounded without tangents and mutterings. Each page shall act as a living document, communicating the current state of the project. They shall explain reasoning for the project, list its primary features, include updated media, and present future work to be done. When there is desire to record challenges, learnings, or regrets, blog posts shall be made and linked to. Projects act as a landing page, not an article.

#### Blogs
Blogs can be more free form than projects! This is where I can relax and share what is on my mind. They may be related to my projects, such as a dev log or a teaching piece. Or they may be arbitrarily abstract and consist of an uncensored stream of consciousness from personal philosophies to my predictions of what is coming next. Or hey, maybe I will share a list of cool kitchen gadgets I found, you just never know.
